Tiny Mighty Brain: a brand built from its story

Brand story, identity and brand book by Marcela Gregory

The brand

Tiny Mighty Brain is an educational app and child development platform for parents, educators and caregivers, with playful activities for phonetics, motor skills, emotions, creativity, nutrition and mindful routines.

The story

The brand starts from one idea: to care for a child well, the adult must also feel cared for. Tiny Mighty Brain is a bridge between generations, supporting the grown-ups as much as the little ones.

The identity

A hand-drawn logo in the shape of a brain, a warm palette, soft shapes and natural photography. Warm, trustworthy, educational, modern and always child-focused.
