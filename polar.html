POLAR: A New Mentality, official guitar playthrough

Art direction, lighting, filming and editing by Marcela Gregory · Released by Arising Empire

The project

The official guitar playthrough of "A New Mentality" by the band POLAR, released on record label Arising Empire's YouTube channel.

My role

I produced the whole video on my own: art direction, lighting design, filming and editing, from the first idea to the final cut.

The look

Cool blue light against warm amber in a dark studio, with the guitarist framed between amp stacks so every eye stays on the playing.
