Macrophylla: a wool brand imagined from the first thread

Brand concept, art direction, photography and post-production by Marcela Gregory

The idea

Macrophylla is a fictional sustainable wool label. I wanted to build a fashion brand end to end: its name, its voice and the stories it would tell.

My role

I created the brand, wrote two visual stories, art-directed and photographed both shoots, retouched every image and designed the lookbook.

Two stories

The Sound of the Breeze plays with lightness and nature. Metamorphosis turns inward, towards a quiet, feline side of self-discovery.
