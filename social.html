Social media · Research · Copywriting · Design

Social content that makes healthy choices simple

Research, copywriting and design by Marcela Gregory

The approach

Two food brands, one goal: turn topics people find confusing, like plant-based nutrition or organic labels, into short, friendly posts they actually save and share.

My role

For both projects I researched the topics, wrote every line of copy and designed the carousels, from the first idea to the final artwork.

The format

Carousels built like a mini story: a bold cover to stop the scroll, one idea per slide, and a clear sign-off with the brand.

Better Than Milk · Go Vegan

2024 · Social content concept

A carousel on how to keep a balanced diet without animal protein, for a plant-based milk brand: three simple tips, each with its own colourful still life.

Planet Organic · Fact Friday

2024 · Spec project

"5 ways to go organic": a weekly educational series for the London organic grocer, mixing everyday tips with facts about farming, animal welfare and soil health.
